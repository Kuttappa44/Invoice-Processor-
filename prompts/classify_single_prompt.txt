A B2B (Business-to-Business) invoice is issued by one registered business to another
registered business for the supply of goods or services. It indicates a transaction
between two businesses — NOT an invoice generated for a guest's hotel stay.

A GUEST HOTEL STAY invoice bills for a specific guest's room occupancy at a property:
guest name, room/folio, stay period (check-in/check-out), room rent for nights stayed.

Read each invoice holistically. Do not rely on a single field or company name alone.

    • Employee or guest may be named, but the invoice is a business-to-business supply
