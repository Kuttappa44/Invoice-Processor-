═══════════════════════════════════════════
You do NOT need all five identity fields to appear on every page.

Strongest case: a different invoice number under Invoice number / Bill number labels, when visible, always means a NEW invoice — even if guest, conf no., room, stay dates, and hotel branding look the same (e.g. split folios).
